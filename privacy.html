Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. We do not share your information with third parties.

Your email address is never shown to other users and all emails you receive from us are sent securely. In addition, we do not send email to multiple recipients. All emails are directed to the individual user's email account.

You may opt-in or opt-out of any email at any time by updating your FBP User Profile.

We are currently investigating the use of text messaging for an additional communication channel. Text messaging is optional and you need to opt-in by providing your cell phone number in your FBP User Profile. As with your email address, we will never show your phone number to other users. You can opt-in or opt-out at any time.
