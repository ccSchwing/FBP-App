Frequently Asked Questions

Is My Personal Information Secure?

Your personal information is secure with FBP. We implement strict security measures to protect your data. Your personal data is never visible to other FBP users. All data stored by FBP is encrypted at rest and in transit, and we follow best practices for data security. For more details, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

Is My Personal Information Private?

All of your personal information is private. Your email address is used by FBP to send you any emails that you have opted into. Your mobile number is used by FBP to send you any text messages that you have opted into. All emails and text messages are sent individually to you and your personal information is never shared with other FBP users. The only public information about you is your Screen Name. All user data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and we follow best practices for data security. For more details, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

ChatBot Tip

FBP does have a chatbot feature that can only answer questions related to FBP. You can ask it questions about FBP, and it will provide you with relevant information. For example, you ask it about a specific NFL team, and it will provide you with information about that teams' record as the Favorite and as the Underdog. You can just type in "Bills" and you'll get the rundown on the Bills record to date. You can ask questions about the FBP Pool, and it will provide you with relevant information. For example, you can ask about your own performance in the pool. Just put in your Display Name, email address or Last Name and you'll get your record to date. You can also ask about other players in the pool, and it will provide you with relevant information. To look up another player's stats, you can put in either the full Display Name or a portion of it. NOTE: Absolutely no personal information other than your Screen Name will be contained in a chatbot response. If you ask a relevant question and don't get a satisfactory response, please reach out to FBP Admin for assistance.

I Forgot My Password. What Should I Do?

Don't panic! You can reset your password by clicking the "Forgot Your Password" link on the login page. Follow the instructions to reset your password and regain access to your account. An email will be sent to the email address that is associated with your account with instructions on how to reset your password. If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, please check your spam or junk folder. If you still have trouble resetting your password, please contact FBP Admin at fbpadmin@my-fbp.com.

Please note that your login id and password are managed and stored in an Amazon Web Service in the cloud. FBP does not have access to your password and cannot reset it for you. You must use the "Forgot Your Password" link to reset your password.

How Do I Set My FBP Profile?

The "Update My Profile" link is on every page in the FBP website. Click that link and you'll see a form where you can set your First and Last Names, your Display Name, and your options to receive emails and/or text messages for Reminders and Grid Sheet availablity. You also set a default algorithm that will be used to make your picks if you don't make picks on any given week. You also can set a default Monday Night Tiebreaker value that will be used if you don't set a tiebreaker value for any given week. Your email address is never shown to other users, but it is used by FBP Admin to send you your weekly grid sheet and reminders if you have those options enabled. Don't forget to click the "Update Profile" button at the bottom of the page to save your profile information.

How Do I Set My Email and Text Messaging Preferences?

The "Update My Profile" link is on every page in the FBP website. Click the "Update My Profile" link and you'll see a form where you can set your email and text messaging preferences for Reminders, Pick Sheet and Grid Sheet availability.

To sign up for text messaging:

  • Enter your mobile phone number in the appropriate field.
  • Click on "Verify" to receive a verification code via text message.
  • Enter the verification code in the provided field.
  • Check the boxes for the types of messages you want to receive.
  • Click the "Update Profile" button at the bottom of the page to save your preferences.

To sign up for email notifications:

  • Check the boxes for the types of emails you want to receive.
  • Click the "Update Profile" button at the bottom of the page to save your preferences.

To opt out of email notifications:

  • Go to the "Update My Profile" page.
  • Uncheck the boxes for the types of emails you no longer want to receive.
  • Click the "Update Profile" button at the bottom of the page to save your preferences.

To opt out of Text Messaging:

  • Uncheck the boxes for the types of messages you no longer want to receive.
  • Click the "Update Profile" button at the bottom of the page to save your preferences.
You may also opt out of text mesaging by replying "STOP" to any text message you receive. This will unsubscribe you to that particular text message. To completely unsubscribe from text messaging, reply "STOP ALL" and you will be unsubscribed completely from all text messages. You may opt in again by clicking the "Update My Profile" link that appears on every page. You will not have to verify your mobile phone number again. You may change your mobile phone number at any time by entering a new mobile phone number in the "Update My Profile" page. You will need to verify this new number.
How do I make my picks for the week?

The "Make My Picks For The Week" link is on every page in the FBP website. Click that link and you'll see a table where you can make your picks for each game. You can click the radio button or on the Team Helmet to make your pick. Don't forget to enter a Monday Night Tiebreaker value as well. If you do not enter a value, your default Monday Night Tiebreaker value will be used. If you have not set a default Monday Night Tiebreaker value, a random value will be generated between 21 and 63. Once you have made your picks, click the "Submit My Picks" button at the bottom of the page to save your picks for the week.

What Other Options Are There for Picks?

At the bottom of the pick sheet table, there are buttons that allow your to pick All Away Teams, All Home Teams, All Favorites, All Underdogs or Random. Try them for yourself and see what they do.

Can I Leave Some Picks Blank?

Yes. You can make as many or as few picks as you want. If you don't make a pick for a game, your default algorithm will be used to make your pick for that game. You can set your default algorithm in your profile settings. You can also leave the Monday Night Tiebreaker blank and your default Monday Night Tiebreaker value will be used for that. If you have not set a default Monday Night Tiebreaker value, a random value will be generated between 21 and 63.

When Am I Able To Make My Picks?

FBP is open for picks from Tuesday Noon EST until 8:00 PM EST on Thursday of each week. Once the pool is closed on Thursday at 8:00 PM EST, you will no longer be able to make or change your picks for that week. You can see the current status of the pool on the home page of the FBP website. If you have any questions about when the pool is open or closed, please contact FBP Admin at fbpadmin@my-fbp.com.

What Is A Spread?

The spread is the point difference set by oddsmakers to even the playing field between two teams. For example, if Team A is favored to win by 7.5 points over Team B, the spread would be -7.5 for Team A and +7.5 for Team B. The winner with the spread is determined by adding the spread to the final score of the game. If you pick Team A, they would need to win by more than 7.5 points for you to win with your pick. If you pick Team B, they would need to lose by less than 7.5 points or win the game outright for you to win with your pick.

What is the Monday Night Tiebreaker?

The Monday Night Tiebreaker is the total number of points scored in the Monday Night Football game each week. You can enter your tiebreaker value on the pick sheet when you make your picks for the week. If you don't enter a tiebreaker value, your default tiebreaker value from your profile will be used. If you have not set a default Monday Night Tiebreaker value, a random value will be generated between 21 and 63.

What if there is a tie?

In the event of a tie in the number of correct picks for the week, the Monday Night Tiebreaker will be used to determine the winner. The player with the closest tiebreaker value to the actual total points scored in the Monday Night Football game will be declared the winner for the week. If there is still a tie after using the tiebreaker, then the tied players will share the win for that week. Each player will get 1/2 of the winnings for that week.

Do You Change The Spreads During the Week?

Absolutely not. The spreads are set on Tuesday when the schedule is released and they do not change for the rest of the week. You can make your picks based on the spreads that are set on Tuesday and you don't have to worry about them changing on you later in the week.

Changing the spreads during the week would be unfair to players who made their picks based on the original spreads.

What is the gridsheet?

The gridsheet is available for viewing AFTER the pool is re-opened on Tuesday. Might sound confusing, but making the gridsheet available when the pool is open for picks could give another player an unfair advantage. So, wait until Tuesday to check the gridsheet. If you try to view it before Tuesday, you'll see a message telling you that the gridsheet will be available when the pool is re-opened on Tuesday.

How can I see the weekly results?

After the pool re-opens on Tuesday, you can view the weekly results by clicking on the "Show Results For the Previous Week" link on any page of the FBP website. If you try to view the weekly results before Tuesday, you'll see a message telling you that the results will be available after the pool re-opens on Tuesday.

I forgot to make my picks. Can you re-open the pool for me?

No — once the pool is closed, it cannot be re-opened for individual players. You must make your picks before the pool closes each week. Your picks will be made for you based on your default algorithm if you don't make them before the pool closes. You can modify your profile to receive email reminders to make your picks.

Do I need to sign out?

That's up to you. If you're on a shared computer, it's a good idea to sign out when you're done. You can sign out by clicking the "Sign Out" link at the top right of any page in the FBP website. If you're on a personal device that only you have access to, you can stay signed in for convenience. If you wait a week or so to come back to the FBP site, you may get redirected to the login page and asked to sign in again. This is a security measure to protect your account.
